Exact certificate

Not an artist’s approximation

This viewer uses the 24 integer-coordinate vertices and eight cyclic nonagonal face walks published in Ruslan Mizhaev’s September 2026 preprint. Each face is triangulated only for GPU rendering; the boundary polygon and incidence structure remain the published ones.

The accompanying verifiers check exact planarity and incidence, reconstruct all 36 undirected edges, confirm orientability and genus, and independently test that all 28 face pairs intersect only in their prescribed shared edge set.

Source discrepancy: the primary arXiv preprint states 36 edges; the September 29, 2026 Popular Science article says 26. This viewer follows the primary coordinate certificate and independently reconstructs 36 unique edges.

Face adjacency

Multiplicity matrix

Every off-diagonal entry is 1 or 2: every pair of faces shares at least one edge. Eight face pairs share two.

Symmetry

T(x,y,z) = (y, −x, −z)

The published realization has a geometric symmetry group isomorphic to C₄. Use T step in the viewer to apply the order-four rotoreflection. After four steps the model returns to its original orientation.

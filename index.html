What is this app? · Ứng dụng này làm gì?

An interactive scientific viewer for exploring and independently checking a newly published genus-3 polyhedron.

Tiếng Việt: Ứng dụng biến bộ tọa độ nguyên và cấu trúc mặt được công bố trong preprint của Ruslan Mizhaev thành mô hình 3D có thể xoay, zoom rất sâu/rất xa và kiểm tra trực quan. Bạn có thể xem từng mặt, từng đỉnh, phương trình mặt phẳng, quan hệ kề, đối xứng C₄ và các bất biến topo như Euler characteristic và genus.

English: It is not a CAD editor or an artist's approximation. It is a reproducible visualization and verification companion for the exact published coordinate certificate.

English: The coordinates and face walks define the exact surface; camera orientation does not. This viewer now uses CAD-style Z-up views and opaque faces by default. “Paper 1–3” are calibrated/recreated directions from the published Figure 1, not a claim of pixel-identical camera metadata.

Figure 2 of the paper explicitly shows F1 as orange and F3 as green; the viewer follows those two source colors and uses a paper-inspired palette for the remaining faces.

Exact certificate

Not an artist’s approximation

This viewer uses the 24 integer-coordinate vertices and eight cyclic nonagonal face walks published in Ruslan Mizhaev’s September 2026 preprint. Each face is triangulated only for GPU rendering; the boundary polygon and incidence structure remain the published ones.

The accompanying verifiers check exact planarity and incidence, reconstruct all 36 undirected edges, confirm orientability and genus, and independently test that all 28 face pairs intersect only in their prescribed shared edge set.

Source discrepancy: the primary arXiv preprint states 36 edges; the September 29, 2026 Popular Science article says 26. This viewer follows the primary coordinate certificate and independently reconstructs 36 unique edges.

Face adjacency

Multiplicity matrix

Every off-diagonal entry is 1 or 2: every pair of faces shares at least one edge. Eight face pairs share two.

Symmetry

T(x,y,z) = (y, −x, −z)

The published realization has a geometric symmetry group isomorphic to C₄. Use T step in the viewer to apply the order-four rotoreflection. After four steps the model returns to its original orientation.
